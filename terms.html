2. What Sayloud does

Sayloud reads PDFs, EPUBs, web articles, scanned pages and pasted text aloud with the current word highlighted, using built-in Apple voices on the device and optional cloud voices. It turns live speech and audio or video files into text on the device (Transcribe), and runs a teleprompter whose script follows your voice and can record video (Prompter). It also has a library, custom pronunciations and a widget.
