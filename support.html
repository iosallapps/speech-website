Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Sayloud, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. This works for subscriptions and for the lifetime purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Sayloud. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Why does a voice not play when I am offline?

Cloud voices are made on a server, so they need an internet connection. Built-in voices run on your iPhone and work offline. Pick a built-in voice to keep listening without a connection.

Which languages can I transcribe?

Sayloud uses Apple's on-device speech recognition, so it can transcribe the languages that your iPhone supports for on-device recognition. Your audio is not sent to a server.

How do I change my privacy choices for ads?

Where your region requires it, open Sayloud's Settings and tap Privacy choices to see Google's consent form again. To change whether apps can ask to track you, open the iOS Settings app, then Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Do I need an account?

No. Sayloud has no account and no sign up. Your documents stay on your iPhone.
