Your pages stay on your phone

Sayloud has no account and no sign up. Here is what stays on your iPhone, and the few cases where something leaves it.

Stays on your iPhone

  • Your documents, imported files and pasted text
  • Your recordings, transcripts and settings
  • Speech recognition and transcription
  • Reading with the built-in voices
  • Text recognition when you scan a page

Leaves your iPhone

  • Cloud voices: the passage being read goes over an encrypted connection to our server and on to Inworld AI, which makes the audio. Prefer it stays put? Use a built-in voice.
  • Web import: the app downloads the page straight from the website.
  • Analytics and crash reports: sent to Google Firebase. Analytics follows your consent choice.
  • Ads and purchases: free users see ads from Google AdMob. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign up. Your documents and settings live on your iPhone.

What can Sayloud read aloud?

PDFs, EPUBs, web articles, scanned pages and text you paste. Each word is highlighted while it is read.

What is the difference between built-in and cloud voices?

Built-in voices are Apple's voices and run on your iPhone. Cloud voices are natural voices for subscribers: the text being read is sent to our server and to our speech provider to make the audio, so they need a connection.

Does transcription happen on my iPhone?

Yes. Live transcription and file transcription use Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio is not sent to us.

Can the Prompter record video?

Yes. It scrolls your script by following your voice and can record video at the same time. Recordings are saved to your photo library.

Is Sayloud free?

Yes, with ads. A weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, removes ads and unlocks the cloud voices.
