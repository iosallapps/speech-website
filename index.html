How it works

  1. 1

    Add something to read

    Open a PDF, EPUB, RTF or text file, paste text, import a web page by its link, or scan a printed page with the camera.

  2. 2

    Listen, or speak

    Press play and follow the highlighted words. Or start a transcription, or open the Prompter and read your script to the camera.

  3. 3

    Keep it or share it

    Everything stays in your library on your phone. Copy or share a transcript, and find a Prompter recording in your photos.

Your pages stay on your phone

Sayloud has no account and no sign up. Here is what stays on your iPhone, and the few cases where something leaves it.

Stays on your iPhone

  • Your documents, imported files and pasted text
  • Your recordings, transcripts and settings
  • Speech recognition and transcription
  • Reading with the built-in voices
  • Text recognition when you scan a page

Leaves your iPhone

  • Cloud voices: the passage being read goes over an encrypted connection from your phone to the speech provider that makes the audio. Prefer it stays put? Use a built-in voice.
  • Web import: the app downloads the page straight from the website.
  • Analytics and crash reports: sent to Google Firebase. Analytics follows your consent choice.
  • Ads and purchases: free users see ads from Google AdMob. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases.

When Sayloud is not the right fit

  • You need it on Android, Windows or the web. Sayloud is for iPhone and iPad.
  • You want a bot that joins your Zoom or Teams calls. Sayloud transcribes what your phone hears and the files you give it.
  • You want natural cloud voices with no internet connection. The built-in voices work offline; cloud voices need a connection.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign up. Your documents and settings live on your iPhone.

What can Sayloud read aloud?

PDFs, EPUBs, web articles, scanned pages and text you paste. Each word is highlighted while it is read.

What is the difference between built-in and cloud voices?

Built-in voices are Apple's voices and run on your iPhone. Cloud voices are natural voices for Pro, with a short free daily allowance: the text being read is sent from your phone to our speech provider to make the audio, so they need a connection.

Does transcription happen on my iPhone?

Yes. Live transcription and file transcription use Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio is not sent to us.

Can the Prompter record video?

Yes. It scrolls your script by following your voice and can record video at the same time. Recordings are saved to your photo library.

Is Sayloud free?

Yes, with ads. A weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, removes ads and unlocks the cloud voices.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for reading with the built-in voices, transcription and the Prompter. Cloud voices and importing web pages need an internet connection.

Which files can I open?

PDF, EPUB, RTF and plain text files, web pages by link, scanned pages and pasted text. For transcription, audio recordings and videos.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Sayloud runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later.

Which languages can it transcribe?

The languages your iPhone supports for on-device speech recognition. Sayloud uses them as they are, without sending audio anywhere.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In the Settings app on your iPhone: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Sayloud. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
