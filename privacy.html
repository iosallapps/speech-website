1. Who we are

Visto Apps is the brand of Darius Cirjan, the developer and data controller. This policy explains what the Sayloud app (iOS) and the website https://sayloudapp.com do with personal data.

Contact for any privacy question or request: iosallapps@gmail.com. Website: vistoapps.com. We answer within one month (art. 12 GDPR). We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer.

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

2. At a glance

  • No account. Sayloud does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your documents, pasted text, transcripts, scripts, recordings and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Personalised ads only with your consent; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland no ad loads before you answer the consent form.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) start only after the consent form has been answered in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Crash reports help us fix bugs.
  • We do not sell personal data for money. We have no servers of our own that receive your data.

3. Data that stays on your device

Documents you import (PDF, EPUB, RTF, text), web pages you import, scanned pages, pasted text, transcripts, Prompter scripts, custom pronunciations and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We have no server that receives them and we cannot see them. Speech recognition for Transcribe and the Prompter's voice follow runs on the device. Title, Summary, Key points and Clean up in the transcript editor use Apple Intelligence on the device where it is available. Audio made by cloud voices is saved on the device so the passage plays again without a connection; Settings, Storage, Delete downloaded audio removes it. You can delete any document in the Library, and deleting the app removes everything. Videos you record in the Prompter are saved to your Photos library, which you manage in Photos. The widget shows what you were listening to, read from storage shared between the app and the widget on your device.

5. Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you use the feature, and works without them. Change them anytime in your device settings.

  • Microphone. Transcribe, and the Prompter's voice follow and video recording. The audio is turned into text on the device and is not sent anywhere.
  • Speech recognition. Apple's recogniser, run on the device. If a language is only recognised on Apple's servers, the app reports it unavailable instead of sending audio.
  • Camera. To scan printed pages (text is recognised on the device, images are not uploaded) and to film you in the Prompter when you turn on Record video.
  • Photo library (add only). To save the videos you record in the Prompter. Sayloud never reads your photos.
  • Files you pick. Only the file you choose to import is read, on the device.
  • Notifications. Local alerts posted by the app itself, for example when a link you saved for import has been fetched. No push server is involved.
  • Tracking (iOS, App Tracking Transparency). Asked once, after the consent form. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it.

6. Consent, ads and tracking

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app first shows Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). Until you answer it, no ad is requested and no usage statistics are sent. If you refuse personalised ads, you still get limited, non-personalised ads.

On iOS the App Tracking Transparency prompt comes after the consent form. Ads load only once it is answered.

Change your choice anytime: in the app, Settings, Support, Privacy choices (shown where Google's consent form applies). On iOS, also Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Buying the paid version removes ads.

7. Purchases

Purchases are made through App Store. The store processes the payment; we receive only a pseudonymous receipt, checked through RevenueCat, that tells the app which purchase you own. We never see your card or account details.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Data held by the services above is kept for the period listed in the table. We do not keep copies of our own. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer, and at most 2 years.

9. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers (Google, RevenueCat and others marked in the table) process data in the United States or other countries. Transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (art. 45 and 46 GDPR).

10. Your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing (including to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Because the app has no account, we usually cannot tell which data at Google, RevenueCat, OpenAI or ElevenLabs is yours. Write to iosallapps@gmail.com and include, if you can, your purchase receipt or the Apple order number, and we will forward the request to the provider. You can always delete all data on the device by deleting the app.

You can complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work, or to ours: Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), dataprotection.ro. The list of EU authorities: edpb.europa.eu.

11. United States

  • We do not sell personal information for money.
  • When you allow personalised ads, Google AdMob uses your advertising identifier for ads based on activity in other apps. Some US state laws (for example California) call this "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time: refuse tracking on iOS (Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking), or email us and we will treat it as an opt-out request.
  • Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws can ask to know, correct or delete their personal information, and will not be treated differently for doing so. Categories collected in the last 12 months are the ones in the table above (identifiers, internet activity, approximate location from IP, purchase records). Requests: iosallapps@gmail.com.

12. Children

Sayloud is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in their country (13 to 16 in the EU). If you believe a child gave us data, write to iosallapps@gmail.com and we will delete what we can reach. Ads in the app are not designed for children.

13. Security

All network connections use HTTPS. Data on the device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and encryption. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep what leaves your device to the minimum listed here.

14. This website

sayloudapp.com is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which records the IP address of visitors in server logs for security. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics, ads or third-party scripts or fonts, and stores nothing in your browser.

15. Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we update this page and the date at the top before the new version ships. Important changes are also mentioned in the store update notes.
