Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Effective date: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sayloud app for iPhone and iPad ("Sayloud" or the "App") handles your data. Sayloud is developed and provided by Cirjan Darius ("we", "us" or "our"), who is the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at iosallapps@icloud.com.

Sayloud has no account and no sign in. It uses one server of our own, only for cloud voices, and services from Inworld AI, Google, RevenueCat and Apple. This policy sets out what each of them receives, why, and how you can control it.

2. Privacy at a glance

  • Your content stays on your device. Documents, imported files, text, recordings, transcripts and settings are stored on your iPhone and are not uploaded to us.
  • Speech recognition runs on your device. Live transcription, file transcription and the Prompter's voice tracking use Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio is not sent to us.
  • Built-in voices run on your device. Cloud voices are the one feature that sends text out: the passage being read goes to our server and to Inworld AI, which turns it into speech.
  • Analytics and crash reports: Google Firebase measures how the App is used and reports crashes. Analytics depends on your consent choice.
  • Ads: the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Subscribers and lifetime buyers do not see ads.
  • Purchases: Apple processes payments, and RevenueCat tells the App which plan you have.
  • We never ask for your name or email address, and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Your content and device permissions

3.1 Content stored on your device

Sayloud keeps the following on your iPhone, and we have no access to it there:

  • Documents and files you import, such as PDFs and EPUBs, web pages you import, scanned pages and text you paste
  • Your library, custom pronunciations and settings
  • Prompter scripts, transcripts and audio you record in the App

None of this is uploaded to us. The only text that leaves your device is the passage you play with a cloud voice, described in section 5. This content can be included in your iCloud or computer backup of your device, which Apple's privacy policy covers.

3.2 Importing a web page

When you import a web page by its link, the App downloads that page directly from the website. The website receives the request, as it would from a browser, including your IP address. We do not receive the link or the page.

3.3 Camera

The camera is used only to scan text from paper. Text recognition runs on your device, and the images are not uploaded.

3.4 Microphone and photo library

  • Microphone: used for dictation, transcription, the Prompter's voice tracking and Prompter video recording. Speech recognition runs on your device, as described in section 4.
  • Photo library: Sayloud asks for add-only access, to save your Prompter recordings. It cannot read the photos and videos already in your library.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Sayloud.

4. Speech recognition and built-in voices

Live transcription, transcription of audio and video files, dictation and the Prompter's voice tracking use Apple's on-device speech recognition. Our code requires on-device mode, so your audio is not sent to Apple's servers, and it is not sent to us.

The built-in voices are Apple's voices and run on your device. Reading with a built-in voice sends nothing out of your device.

5. Cloud voices

Cloud voices are natural voices available to subscribers and lifetime buyers. When you play text with a cloud voice:

  • The passage being read is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to Inworld AI, our speech provider, and returns the audio to the App.
  • Our server does not store the text.
  • Our server keeps usage counts linked to an anonymous identifier for your installation of the App, and your subscription status, so that it can apply the limits of your plan and prevent abuse.
  • Inworld AI processes the text to produce speech and may retain it under its own privacy policy. We do not have a zero-data-retention agreement with Inworld AI, so we cannot promise that the text is deleted as soon as the audio is made.

If you do not want any text sent from your device, use the built-in voices. They work without cloud processing.

6. Services in Sayloud

The services below receive data directly from the App. None of them receive your documents, recordings or transcripts, and none of them receive your name or email address.

6.1 Advertising: Google AdMob

The free version of Sayloud shows ads served by Google AdMob. Subscribers and lifetime buyers do not see ads. Google receives information such as your IP address, from which it can estimate your approximate location, device and app information, ad requests and interactions, and, only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices about ads.
  • Tracking: iOS asks, through App Tracking Transparency, whether Sayloud may use your advertising identifier. If you do not allow it, ads are not personalised using the advertising identifier. Google may still use contextual information, such as the app the ad appears in and your approximate location.

Google's privacy policy applies to the data it receives: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6.2 Usage analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

Google Analytics for Firebase receives events that describe how the App is used, such as which screens and features you open, together with an identifier for your installation of the App, device and app information, and your IP address, from which Google estimates your approximate location. We use this to understand which features work and to fix what does not.

Analytics depends on your consent choice. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you make that choice in the consent form shown in the App.

6.3 Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

When the App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a report with the technical details of the crash, an identifier for your installation of the App, and device and app information, such as the device model, iOS version and app version. We use these reports to find and fix problems.

Firebase is provided by Google. More about how Firebase handles data is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6.4 Purchases: Apple and RevenueCat

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment, and your payment details never reach us or RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user identifier created for your installation of the App and your App Store purchase information, such as which plan you have and when it renews or expires. We use this to know which plan is active, to restore purchases and to apply the limits of your plan.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy, and Apple's is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

6.5 Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use them only to answer you.

7. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Consent for analytics, for advertising where consent is required, and for tracking through your advertising identifier. You give it in the consent form and in the iOS tracking prompt, and you can withdraw it at any time, as described in section 12. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.
  • Contract for producing cloud voice audio when you play text with a cloud voice, and for processing your purchase and plan status through Apple and RevenueCat, which is needed to provide what you bought.
  • Legitimate interests for crash reports, for the usage counts our server keeps to apply plan limits and prevent abuse, and for answering your emails. You can object to this processing by contacting us.

8. Tracking and App Tracking Transparency

iOS asks whether Sayloud may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, your advertising identifier is available to Google AdMob, subject to your choices in the consent form where one applies. If you ask Sayloud not to track, the advertising identifier is not used.

You can change your answer at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

9. Children's privacy

Sayloud is not directed at children under the age of 13, and you must be at least 13 to use it, as our Terms of Service say. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used Sayloud and data about them has been collected, contact us at iosallapps@icloud.com and we will help have it deleted.

10. International data transfers

Google, RevenueCat and Inworld AI may process data in the United States and in other countries. When data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, these companies protect it with safeguards recognised under GDPR, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. How long data is kept

  • Content on your device stays until you delete it. Deleting the App removes Sayloud's data from your device.
  • Our server does not store the text you play with cloud voices. It keeps usage counts and subscription status for as long as they are needed to apply plan limits and prevent abuse.
  • Inworld AI keeps text it receives under its own policy, as described in section 5.
  • Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob keep data for the periods set out in Google's privacy information, linked in section 6.
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as they are needed to provide your plan and to meet accounting and legal obligations.
  • Emails are kept for as long as needed to answer you and follow up, and then deleted.

12. Your rights and choices

12.1 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to a data protection authority, for example in the country where you live or, in Romania, the ANSPDCP at dataprotection.ro. We would appreciate the chance to help first.

12.2 Controls in Sayloud and iOS

  • Consent form: where your region requires it, reopen Google's consent form in Sayloud's Settings, under Privacy choices.
  • Tracking: iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Cloud voices: choose a built-in voice to keep all reading on your device.
  • Camera, microphone and photos: iOS Settings, under Sayloud.
  • Everything on your device: delete the App. This does not cancel a subscription.

12.3 Requests about data held by the services

Because Sayloud has no accounts, the data held by our server, Google and RevenueCat is linked to random identifiers rather than to your name or email address, so we often cannot tell which records are yours. Email us at iosallapps@icloud.com and we will do what we can. For purchase records, include the order ID from your Apple receipt, which you can find at reportaproblem.apple.com. You can manage Google ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com. We answer privacy requests within one month.

12.4 California residents

We do not sell personal information. If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier and ad data that Google receives may count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. You can stop this at any time by not allowing tracking in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking. You have the right to know about and delete your personal information and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

13. Security

Your content stays on your device, protected by iOS app sandboxing and device encryption. Cloud voice requests and the services in section 6 use encrypted connections. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, but we share only what this policy describes. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Sayloud or the services it uses change. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top, and if a change materially affects you, we will tell you in the App or in the App Store update notes before it takes effect. The current policy is always at sayloudapp.com/privacy.html.
